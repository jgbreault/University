## Week 4

Summary:
Developed and implemented a GIS algorithm to label lot boundaries as front, side, or rear.

Tasks:
- Wrote reports to outline a robust lot line labelling algorithm that captures lots of any shape.
- Implemented the lot line labelling algorithm in QGIS for 1000 Burnaby lots. 
- Facilitated communication and planning for future capstone logistics.

Time:
- Writing Lot Line Labelling Reports: 10 hours
- Developing QGIS Lot Line Labelling Solution: 25 hours
- DATA 599 Presentation: 1 hour
- Meetings: 2 hours
Total: 38 hours

Challenges:

- QGIS crashed on me twice, and progress was lost, so progress will be saved very frequently going forward.
