# Weekly Presentations

During weekly **in-person** meetings with your mentor, your team will provide a brief update on your project progress. Unless otherwise specified, these presentations should be 10–12 minutes in length and followed by discussion and feedback.

Your presentation should clearly communicate what your team has accomplished, what you have learned, and what you plan to do next. Slide decks must be committed to your project repository **prior** to your scheduled meeting. For ease of reference, please _number your slides_.

The following provides a general structure for your presentation:

1. **Project overview (1 slide)**
   - A high-level summary of your project (this slide can be reused and updated throughout the term)

2. **Progress update**
   - Work completed during the current cycle  
   - Key findings or preliminary results  
   - What has been successful and what has not  

3. **Challenges and roadblocks**
   - Any issues encountered  
   - Steps taken (or planned) to address them  

4. **Next steps**
   - Planned tasks for the upcoming cycle  
   - Clear assignment of responsibilities to team members  

5. **Project tracking**
   - Progress relative to your original plan and timeline  
   - Any deviations and justification  

6. **Client communication**
   - Summary of recent interactions with your client  

7. **Team contributions**
   - Summary of individual contributions and total hours  

> **Note**: Presentation time should be distributed equitably among team members.

These meetings are an opportunity to receive feedback on both your technical progress and your overall project approach. Teams are expected to come prepared and use feedback to guide subsequent work.
