# Logging Hours and Contributions

Maintaining accurate and consistent logs of your work is an important part of the capstone project. Logs are used to support individual assessment, ensure transparency within teams, and help identify progress, challenges, and workload distribution.

## Expectations

Each team member is required to:

- Record their contributions **weekly**
- Track **time spent** on project-related tasks
- Submit logs via Pull Request by the stated deadline
- Ensure logs are reviewed and approved by all team members

Logs should be **clear, honest, and specific**. They should reflect actual work completed during the week.

---

## What to Include

Your weekly log should include:

### 1. Summary of Work
A brief description of what you worked on during the week.

Example:

- Cleaned and merged survey datasets  
- Created initial visualizations of response distributions  
- Met with team to refine analysis plan  

---

### 2. Tasks Completed
List specific tasks and deliverables.

Example:

- Implemented data cleaning script in R  
- Drafted 3 slides for weekly presentation  
- Reviewed teammate’s pull request  

---

### 3. Time Spent
Provide an estimate of time spent on each task and total hours for the week.

Example:

- Data cleaning: 4 hours  
- Visualization: 3 hours  
- Meetings: 2 hours  
**Total: 9 hours**

---

### 4. Challenges / Blockers
Note any issues encountered and how you addressed them (or plan to address them).

Example:

- Difficulty interpreting missing values in dataset  
- Waiting on client clarification  

---

### 5. Next Steps
Outline what you plan to work on next week.

Example:

- Finalize preprocessing pipeline  
- Begin exploratory modeling  

---

## Best Practices

- **Be specific** — vague logs (e.g., “worked on project”) are not helpful  
- **Be consistent** — update logs regularly (not all at once at the end of the week)  
- **Be honest** — logs are used to support fair individual assessment  
- **Keep it concise** — clear bullet points are preferred over long paragraphs  
- **Align with team progress** — your log should reflect your role in the team’s work  

---

## Example Log Entry

```text
Week 3

Summary:
Worked on data preprocessing and initial exploratory analysis.

Tasks:
- Cleaned missing values in dataset (R script)
- Created histograms for key variables
- Attended team meeting and contributed to planning

Time:
- Data cleaning: 3 hours
- Visualization: 2 hours
- Meeting: 1 hour
Total: 6 hours

Challenges:
- Inconsistent formatting in raw data

Next Steps:
- Finalize cleaning pipeline
- Start regression analysis
```
